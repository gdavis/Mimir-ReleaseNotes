## New Features
- **Podcast Seasons** — For podcasts that publish seasons, browse by season, play or queue an entire season, and choose a season as a Continuous Playback source.

## Improvements
- **Episode List Header** — See how many episodes are shown versus the total when a filter, search, or season narrows the list, along with a clearer filter and sort caption.

## Bug Fixes
- Fixed a crash that could occur while the library was updating in the background.
- Fixed blank refresh buttons on iOS 17.
