## Improvements
- **Episode Show Notes** — Show notes now use the podcast's full formatted description, with tidier spacing, preserved line breaks, and tappable links.

## Bug Fixes
- Fixed episode descriptions being cut off and not scrolling on the episode detail and Now Playing screens.
- Fixed cloud sync changing your position in an episode you were actively listening to.
- Fixed navigation and cramped spacing on the Release Notes screen.
- Fixed a crash that could happen when an episode had an empty description.
- Fixed a crash that could happen while Now Playing updated in the background.
- Fixed the size of the continuous playback header in Up Next.
