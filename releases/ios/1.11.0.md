## New Features
- **Episode Chapters** — Downloaded episodes with embedded chapter markers now show a Chapters tab in episode info; tap a chapter to jump to that point in the episode.
- **Episode Artwork** — Downloaded episodes that include their own artwork now show that picture on the episode screen, Now Playing, and Lock Screen / Control Center, instead of always using the podcast cover.
- **Chapter Artwork** — When a downloaded episode includes pictures for individual chapters, Now Playing and Lock Screen / Control Center update as you listen.
- **Track Buttons** — Choose whether Bluetooth next and previous buttons skip by your usual interval or move to the next episode and restart the current one.
- **OPML Groups** — Importing an OPML file now recreates nested podcast groups, and exporting includes your library folders instead of a flat list of feeds.

## Improvements
- **Episode Show Notes** — Links in episode descriptions follow your theme tint, open reliably from episode info, and formatted lists display more cleanly.
- **Episode Info Layout** — The Description/Chapters selector is easier to read and aligns with the episode content.
